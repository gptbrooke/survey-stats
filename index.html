    Methodology & honest caveats

    Where this comes from. After each event, every attendee is emailed an exit survey — the core questions have stayed the same since Vibecamp 2, with small tweaks from year to year. Responses are anonymous and optional, which means this data describes the campers who chose to answer — a group that may differ from attendees as a whole.

    Sample sizes matter. Response counts range from — (Vibeclipse) to — (Vibecamp 2), and response rates vary a lot by year. Every chart on this page shows its n; anything built on fewer than ~10 responses is marked as a small sample and worth a grain of salt. Vibeclipse's chart-topping scores come from just 38 respondents at an unusual event (there was a total solar eclipse) — enjoy them, don't bank on them.

    Vibecamp 1 had no quantitative questions. The 2022 exit survey was a completely different format, so VC1 appears only in the “what people said” section.

    The quotes are paraphrased. Free-text answers are summarized and paraphrased — never quoted verbatim — so no camper can be identified by their words. Themes were distilled by reading every response.

    Privacy. This page is built from pre-aggregated counts only. No individual survey responses are published here or in this site's source repository, and comparisons are shown one dimension at a time so small groups can't be cross-identified.
